Hotel Booking Dashboard

Project Overview
This project presents an Excel dashboard for exploring hotel booking activity and cancellations. It summarizes booking volume and compares cancellation patterns across hotel types, guest types, room preferences, and months.

Business Objective
The objective is to understand how often bookings are cancelled and explore how cancellation counts vary across different booking categories. These insights can help hotel teams identify patterns that may deserve further investigation.

Tool Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel charts and dashboard visualizations
- Date/year filter (as shown in the dashboard)

Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard displays the following headline figures and comparisons:
- Total bookings: 119,390
- Total cancellations: 44,224
- Overall cancellation rate: approximately 37.0% (44,224 ÷ 119,390)

It also includes visualizations for:
- Guest type: comparison of Couples, Family, and Single guest categories
- Room preference: comparison of Desired and Undesired room types
- Monthly trends: total guests/bookings and cancelled bookings across January to December
- Year filter: a slicer for selecting 2015, 2016, or 2017

Key Observations
Based on the values visible in the dashboard:
1. The dashboard reports 119,390 total bookings and 44,224 cancellations, giving an overall cancellation rate of approximately 37.0%.
2. In the guest-type chart, the Couples category has the highest displayed total (81,560) and the highest cancellation count (32,424) among the three guest categories shown.
3. The Desired room category has a much higher displayed booking count (104,473) than the Undesired category (14,917).
4. The monthly chart shows variation in booking and cancellation counts throughout the year. August appears to have the highest displayed total in the monthly series, while cancellation counts also vary by month.
